# Design Direction

Visual principles and references. General direction lives in `CLAUDE.md` section 5.

## Approved decisions
- Sidebar: floating glass panel on the right, detached from the edge, rounded corners.
- Typography: Inter for titles, Geist Mono for labels and numbers (font files supplied by the user).
- Accent: one restrained accent, used only for active or focused states. Everything else is greyscale.

## References
- `references/layout 1.jpg`: floating panel, faint dot canvas, one indigo accent, sans type.
- `references/layout 2.jpg`: docked card-based sidebar, dense dot grid, monospace uppercase labels, no accent.

## Shared patterns
- Near-black background with a subtle dot grid.
- Light artwork on a dark ground, centered, clean edges, no heavy shadow.
- Slightly lifted dark panels with hairline borders and 12–16 px corners.
- Controls grouped under titled sections with thin dividers.
- Compact density, small type, near-greyscale interface.
- Live feedback, with no apply step.
